Merchant Weight Rounding Metafield Update

Add Weight Rounding Metafield

  • Navigate to the Metafields page in Shopify
  • (Go to "Settings" down the bottom left, click on "Metafields and metaobjects" in the lower left)
  • Click on "Products" to view the Product Metafields
  • Remove the existing "weight rounding value" or "weight rounding to nearest grams" Product Metafield
  • (Click on the Metafield, select "More actions", chose "Delete Definition" and select "Delete field and its saved values" if prompted)
  • Go back to the Products Metafields page if necessary
  • Add the following Product Metafield Definition:
    • Start by clicking "Add definition" if you haven't already.
    • Set the "Name" to "weight rounding"
    • Set the "Type" to "JSON"
    • Click "Save" to save the new Metafield.


Update Product Weight Rounding Metafield

  • Go to the "Products" page, search for and select the "aluminium-plate-test" product
  • Scroll down to "Product metafields", click the "weight rounding" field and enter the following: 
    {"value_grams":500,"weight_product_variant_id":49215967265012}
  • Click "Save" up the top, to save the Product


  • All done.
